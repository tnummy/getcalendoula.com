Calendoula Staff SMS Program

Program disclosure for work-related text notifications sent by Calendoula, the scheduling & business-management platform (getcalendoula.com).

What this program is

Calendoula sends work-related text notifications to staff members of the businesses that use the Calendoula platform: appointment & schedule alerts, account alerts, and login verification codes. Staff who separately opt in may also receive occasional Calendoula product updates & tips.

How staff members opt in (mobile opt-in path)

  1. A business using Calendoula invites a staff member by email. The email contains a one-time, token-secured link to the account-setup page (the page is unique per invitation, which is why it has no fixed public URL).
  2. On the account-setup page, the staff member creates their login and may optionally enter a mobile phone number.
  3. SMS consent is collected by checkbox, unchecked by default and not required to create or use the account:
Text me work notifications — appointment & schedule alerts, account alerts, and login verification codes — from Calendoula.

A second, separate optional checkbox (also unchecked by default) covers marketing:

I'd like to receive marketing texts from Calendoula — occasional product updates & tips.

The form displays this disclosure beneath the checkboxes:

Consent is not required to use your account. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. We will not share your mobile information with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes. See our Privacy Policy and Terms.

Screenshot of the opt-in form

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on workplace activity (for example, schedule changes, appointment assignments, and login verifications).

Fees

Message and data rates may apply according to the recipient's mobile plan.

Opting out (STOP)

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. You will receive a final confirmation that no further messages will be sent. Reply START to resubscribe. Staff can also disable text notifications in their Calendoula profile settings.

Help (HELP)

Reply HELP to any message, email support@getcalendoula.com, or visit getcalendoula.com.

Privacy

The program's privacy policy is at getcalendoula.com/privacy. Mobile numbers and SMS consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Terms of service: getcalendoula.com/terms.
